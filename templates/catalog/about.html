Hey there! I'm the creator of CV Forge.

As a junior developer entering the tech industry, I faced the exact same problem every job seeker encounters: **creating a clean, properly formatted resume is a nightmare**. I spent hours fighting with text editors, adjusting margins, and worrying if my CV would pass automated applicant tracking systems (ATS).

That's when I decided to turn this frustration into action. I built this tool as a personal project to solve my own problem—and soon realized it could help thousands of other candidates who want to focus on their skills rather than fighting with document layouts.

"My goal is simple: to provide a straightforward, no-nonsense CV generator that builds clean, professional, and employer-ready resumes in minutes. No hidden fees, no messy design choices—just what works."

— The Developer

What makes this project different?

  • Built by a peer: I know exactly what you need because I am going through the same career-building journey.
  • Clean Code & Layouts: The templates focus strictly on readability and what recruiters actually want to see.
  • Constantly Improving: As I grow as a developer, I regularly update this app with new features and suggestions from users.
